ForgeFit — workout summary, movement logging and expanded exercise options

The previous woman/man/drill-sergeant voices are retained.
Built-in exercise illustrations, external photo/YouTube links, equipment,
independent position choices, workout alternatives, food, progress, history,
and backups are retained.

NEW FEATURES
1. Finish a workout to view the summary. Enter actual workout minutes, body
   weight in pounds, and the activity/effort that best describes your session.
   Calculate & save calories stores estimated active and total calories with
   that workout. Your weight is remembered for next time. Estimates are not
   measured calories. The app does not treat the planned duration as actual.
   Reopen the summary from History to view or adjust the estimate.
2. Log movement accepts time in minutes, distance in miles, or both. Decimal
   entries are supported and saved in History. Existing time-only records work.
3. Exactly 100 strength options: combinations spanning different muscle groups
   and including a multi-joint stage. Original movements remain in the library;
   isolation-only pairings now include an additional multi-joint stage.
4. Exactly 100 cardio options: 20 movement patterns with five clearly named
   steady/interval formats. Each has instructions and built-in illustrations.
   Interval cues describe the format; they are not an automatic interval timer.
